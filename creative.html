Every idea has to pass the set test.

Our producers sit in the creative review. If an idea can't survive these three questions, it doesn't reach your inbox.

PASSQ.01

Can we shoot it?

Budget, permits, locations and talent checked before the concept is presented — not after it is approved.

PASSQ.02

Can we stage it?

If the idea works on screen but dies in a mall atrium, it is only half an idea. We build for both.

PASSQ.03

Does it land in Arabic first?

Campaigns here are read in Arabic. If the line only works in English, we start again.
